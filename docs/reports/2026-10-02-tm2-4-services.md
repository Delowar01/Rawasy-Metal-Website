## 7. Dynamic route architecture

`src/app/(commerce)/[locale]/services/[slug]/page.tsx`:

- `generateStaticParams` gives the two locales × the six slugs: 12 prerendered pages.
- `dynamicParams = true`, as in 1D.
- `generateMetadata` is 1D's: the service name and summary, `noindex` while `service` is not published.
- An unknown slug, or an invalid locale, calls `notFound()`.

`services/[slug]/not-found.tsx` re-exports `(missing)/not-found`. An unknown slug therefore gets the Modern Commerce
localized 404 (item 38).

- **Why there:** a boundary sits in the payload of every page under it, so the six service pages carry it:
  ≈ 49 KB raw / 11 KB gzip resolved, of 147–165 KB payloads.
- **What does not carry it:** the homepage, the overview and every other page. Their prerendered files are unchanged
  (item 40).
- **Why not higher:** a boundary beside `services/page.tsx` would have put it in the overview's payload too.

## 51. Deferred shared-polish items

These are frozen, shared parts, not changed in this batch, for your decision:

1. **The homepage's colour switch** shows no on/off state in forced colours (TM-2.3). Clients draws its own.
2. **`PageHero` fades its text in after the script starts** (TM-2.3). LCP is about 1–1.4 s on every Modern Commerce

   of them.

   at an anchor jumps smoothly, and a web font arriving mid-jump leaves it short (item 29). This affects every Modern
   Commerce page; Contact's `#location` too. Proposed fix: enable smooth scrolling only after the first load (the boot
   script or the motion controller adds a class once the page has settled).
5. **New: the phone menu sheet's last row** reads as partly obscured to axe while the open Services list pushes it below
   the sheet's fold. It is reachable by scrolling the sheet, and the same finding appears on the approved homepage and
   About. Proposed fix: let the sheet scroll the opened list into view, or reserve space at its bottom.
